Ecosystem Modules

Built for the Future
of Real World Assets

AgricFi brings together technology, finance, and farming expertise to create a transparent, sustainable, and high-impact agricultural finance network.

01

DeFi Funding Hub

Decentralized pooling of global capital for verified African farms. On-chain escrow, dynamic APYs based on harvest performance.

02

Sustainability Ledger

Proof-of-impact layer recording carbon credits and farm sustainability data immutably on-chain.

03

$AFT Token

Native Solana SPL token powering governance, staking, marketplace fees and automated reward distribution.

04

Yield Dashboard

Real-time IoT data, on-chain analytics, and portfolio performance tracking in one premium investor interface.

05

Farm Title NFTs

Each farm issued as a Metaplex NFT on Solana with GPS coordinates, fractional co-ownership and satellite verification.

06

Marketplace

Trade farm title NFTs, produce tokens, and carbon credits in a live, secure, on-chain marketplace.
